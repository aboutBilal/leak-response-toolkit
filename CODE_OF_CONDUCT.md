# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and remember that people often arrive here during a stressful incident. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form, or by contacting the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
